Coinly — Crypto Dashboard

A mobile app that makes tracking, analysing and trading crypto feel calm instead of chaotic. I led research, interaction design and the visual system across five core flows.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Timeline
12 weeks · 2026
Tools
Stitch, Figma, Tailwind

The problem

New investors felt overwhelmed by dense exchange UIs and abandoned onboarding within the first session.

lightbulb

The approach

Progressive disclosure, a calm dark palette and one primary action per screen guided users from glance to trade.

trending_up

The outcome

+38% onboarding completion and 2.1× weekly active users within three months of launch.
